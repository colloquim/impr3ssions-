Young people designing the mental health solutions they need.

Impr3ssions is built by researchers, for researchers. We support youth aged 18-24 to move ideas from early concept to tested, evidence-informed interventions grounded in local culture.

Learn about us Meet the team

A large gap between need and care

Recent national data shows many young Kenyans struggle, yet few reach support. Much research is developed elsewhere, and young people are rarely invited to design and test solutions.

44%

of adolescents in Kenya experience mental health challenges

12%

meet the threshold for a diagnosable disorder

11%

seek any form of support

2%

access specialized mental health care

Mission

To empower young people to design, test and implement culturally sensitive, ethical, evidence-based mental health interventions that respond to real community needs and improve psychosocial wellbeing.

Vision

A future where youth lead the creation of accessible, culturally relevant mental health solutions that move from ideas to real impact.

What makes Impr3ssions distinct

Youth-led, not youth-targeted

Youth are designers, researchers and implementers.

Evidence before scale

Piloted, measured and refined before expansion.

Incubation-to-action

We close the gap between research and implementation.

Context-responsive

Built within Kenyan and local cultural realities.
