About

A focused research and intervention incubator.

Impr3ssions is a space for our team to design and conduct youth-led research, and a platform where other young people develop their own projects and collaborations. We are not a general youth hub, therapy centre or advocacy platform.

Our five-stage pathway

Cohort-based, with each cycle producing a defined number of pilot-tested projects and documented outputs.

1

Onboarding & foundation

Core research skills, mental health in local cultural contexts, and ethics.

2

Idea development & design

Refine ideas into researchable concepts with clear questions and methods.

3

Mentorship & review

One-on-one mentorship, peer feedback, and ethical and methodological review.

4

Pilot & implementation

Small-scale pilots in real settings, with data collected and documented.

5

Evaluation & pathways

Analyse findings, produce reports or models, and explore scale or partnership.
